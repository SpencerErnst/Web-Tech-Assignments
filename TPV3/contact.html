Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are your price estimates?

Our price estimates are based on current market data from multiple sources including Steam Community Market, Buff 163, and other third-party trading platforms. While we strive for accuracy, the CS:GO skin market is highly volatile and prices can fluctuate significantly.

For high-value items, we recommend checking multiple sources before making investment decisions. Our estimates should be used as general guidelines rather than exact valuations.

How often is the market data updated?

Our market data is updated on a weekly basis to reflect current trends and price movements. For rapidly changing markets or during major events (game updates, operations, tournaments), we may provide more frequent updates.

The "last updated" timestamp at the bottom of market analysis pages indicates when the information was last refreshed.

Do you offer personalized investment advice?

This website provides general market analysis and information for educational purposes. While we don't currently offer personalized investment consultations, you can contact us through the form above with specific questions.

Remember that all investments carry risk, and the CS:GO skin market is particularly volatile. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

How do I get started with CS:GO skin trading?

Getting started with CS:GO skin trading involves several steps:

  1. Set up a Steam account and secure it with Steam Guard
  2. Learn about different skin types, wear values, and pricing factors
  3. Start with a modest budget to gain experience
  4. Familiarize yourself with reputable trading platforms
  5. Research market trends before making significant investments

Check out our beginner's guide video on the homepage for more detailed information.

Are CS:GO skin investments safe?

Like any investment, CS:GO skins carry inherent risks. Market values can fluctuate based on factors including game updates, player base changes, and external economic conditions.

Key considerations for safety include:

  • Only trade through official and reputable platforms
  • Be wary of scams and phishing attempts
  • Diversify your investments across different item types
  • Stay informed about upcoming game changes
  • Consider the potential impact of CS2 and future game developments
